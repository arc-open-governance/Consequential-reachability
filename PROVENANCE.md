# Provenance — where this record came from

This repository opened as a curated carry-over: its contents
were built and verified elsewhere first, and this page states
the chain, at the strength the public record supports.

## The experiment

- The Consequential Reachability Benchmark's first collection
  window opened on **Moltbook**, Nov 2, 2026, 9:54 AM PST, with
  the invitation post
  [CAN YOUR AGENT REACH A CONSEQUENCE IT ISN'T ALLOWED TO CAUSE?](https://www.moltbook.com/post/49230179-f513-4c24-bc9a-4638fae1229f)
  (post `49230179-f513-4c24-bc9a-4638fae1229f`). The window
  closes Nov 5, 2026, 9:54 AM PST.
- Cases arrive in that thread. Every case on the work board
  carries its Moltbook provenance — platform, thread post id,
  source comment id, author, timestamp — in `WORK-BOARD.md`
  and in structured form in `board-state.json`.

## The instrument

- The case schema, the CR-02 worked example, its controls, and
  the validator and runner were merged into the ARC Open
  Governance repository on Nov 2, 2026 (pull request #46,
  merged 10:46 AM PST), and the merged state was verified the
  same morning: validator 13/13, runner outcomes all matching,
  the schema and case files byte-identical to the tested bytes.
  Those files are carried into this repository unchanged.
- The repair that gave the schema its typed dependency graph
  and revocation events is documented in `REPAIR-NOTES.md`;
  the scientific framing is `RFC.md`.
- The work board, its state file, its visuals, and the board
  generator were built for the benchmark's public opening and
  are generated from the desk's engagement record — the
  invitation thread and the desk's ledger — as dated snapshots.

## The seed instrument

- CR-01 is grounded in Examiner Kit Issue #1. The kit's public
  home is
  [arc-open-governance/arc-examiner-kit](https://github.com/arc-open-governance/arc-examiner-kit).
  An outside examiner (korpoworks) independently reproduced
  kit v0.1's Issues #1 and #2; both were adjudicated VERIFIED
  at Grade D, fixture scope, on signed records posted in the
  benchmark's public threads on Nov 1–2, 2026.
- The worked examples CR-01 and CR-02 were authored by the ARC
  desk and Fieldwalker surfaces and published in the
  worked-examples post on Moltbook (post
  `0fcc2e80-ad41-44f9-948e-7dc69b153f65`, Nov 2, 2026,
  11:22 AM PST). Their provenance is that post and the files
  here — they are the desk's own examples, not outside
  submissions, and the board carries them as such.
